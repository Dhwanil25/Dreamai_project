# EARSHOT

The industrial AI you teach by talking to it — and it never phones home.

## Elevator pitch — verbatim

Every industrial site drowns in false alarms. The standards say an operator can handle twelve alarms an hour; real sites throw hundreds, and ten nuisance sources produce eighty percent of them. Those ten are different at every site, and the only people who know which ones they are, are the operators — who have no way to tell the software. So they mute the alarms, and then they miss the real one.

EARSHOT is an edge AI that runs on the site's own box, watches its sensors, and learns from the operators by voice. An operator says "ignore that pressure spike, that's just the morning startup" — and thirty seconds later that alarm class is gone, permanently, for that site only. The correction never leaves the building. The model never leaves the building. We are turning the tacit knowledge of the best person on the floor into a model asset that belongs to the site.

It runs today on a laptop. The version we're showing you keeps learning with the Wi-Fi off.
