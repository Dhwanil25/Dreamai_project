    FROM A SENTENCE TO A SAVED CORRECTION
    What “teaching” actually means.
    You supply the operating judgment. EARSHOT turns it into a validated scope, updates local learning, and shows the effect on real observations.
    
  
    1
    Observe the site
    The replay merges recorded alarms and sensor snapshots in time order. A recent observation buffer gives the correction real examples to learn from.
    2
    Say what should change
    Name the alarm class and the asset. For example: “ignore generator cut-in on turbine four.” Text and microphone input share the same teaching path.
    3
    Validate the instruction
    The parser resolves the instruction against the site’s known turbine IDs and alarm codes. Ambiguous or unsupported instructions do not become broader rules.
    4
    Learn locally, then rescore
    A scoped rule changes matching alarm visibility. A separate River logistic classifier updates from buffered examples. The rule, examples and receipt are saved locally.
    5
    Check the next event. Keep Undo.
    Later matching observations use the saved policy. Undo revokes that correction and reconstructs the classifier from the remaining training batches.
